Digital Sharpening Guide

A magnet-backed angle guide for your knives. Stick it to the blade, set a target angle, and the screen holds green while you hold the bevel. Flash it to your M5StickC Plus, Plus2, or M5StickS3 straight from this page — no toolchain, just a USB-C cable.

TOO LOW — RAISE THE SPINE ON ANGLE TOO HIGH — LOWER IT

01 Pick your device

The M5StickC Plus build is validated on real hardware. The Plus2 and S3 builds are compile-verified and code-reviewed but not yet confirmed on a physical device — if you flash one, please open an issue with how it went.

02 Before you flash

  1. Use Chrome, Edge, or Opera on a desktop or laptop — Web Serial isn't supported on Safari, Firefox, or phones.
  2. Connect the device with a USB-C data cable. Some cables are charge-only; if nothing shows up, try another cable.
  3. Still nothing? Install the CP210x USB driver and reconnect. (The M5StickS3 uses native USB and usually needs no driver.)
This browser can't flash over USB. Open this page in desktop Chrome, Edge, or Opera. Flashing needs a secure (https) connection.

When the dialog opens, pick the serial port — usually CP2104 / USB Serial on a Plus or Plus2, USB JTAG/serial on an S3 — and choose Install.

03 After flashing

  1. The device reboots into a SHARPENING GUIDE splash (it should say v1.0.2), then SET TARGET. A red WRONG FIRMWARE screen means you picked the build for a different stick — go back to step 01.
  2. Stick it onto the knife's flat — the magnet is in the back — and follow the on-screen calibration.
  3. The full walkthrough and control reference are in the README.

◆ What's new

v1.0.2

  • Re-zero now does what the screen says. During an in-place re-zero, tapping B force-captures the reference — exactly as the "tap B to capture" hint promises. (It used to silently cancel instead.) To cancel a re-zero, hold A.
  • No duplicate history entries. If the device went to sleep while showing the session summary, the finished session could be offered for "resume" and end up recorded twice. A finished session now stays finished.
  • An abandoned calibration can't drain the battery. A capture that never completes (say the device ends up jostling around in a bag) used to hold the device awake indefinitely. It now gives up after a minute and lets the normal auto-sleep take over.
  • No stray beep at session start. With the buzzer on, the first moment of a session no longer beeps as if you'd just fallen off the angle.
  • Sleep keeps your stats. Session time and time-on-angle accumulated since your last stroke are no longer lost when the device sleeps mid-session (idle or power button).
  • Also: the accuracy-check screen no longer flickers, the buzzer on/off overlay no longer leaves a black box behind, the WRONG FIRMWARE screen dims to protect the battery, and Plus2 owners get their own diagnostic build.
Earlier releases

v1.0.1

  • Battery icon no longer reads empty at boot. The power chip's sensor is switched on moments before the first reading, so a fully charged device could show a flat battery for its first ten seconds. It now shows a dash until a real reading arrives (about a second).

v1.0.0

  • Battery icon in the top-right corner of every screen — four bars, a + while charging, a dash if the reading isn't available. Polled every ten seconds so it doesn't touch battery life.
  • 1.0 — the calibrate / hold-the-colour / count / summary loop had been stable for months, so this release stopped calling itself a preview.

v0.3.0

  • Steady mode — the angle reading no longer jumps with each stroke. On by default; hold B on the TOLERANCE screen to switch it off and compare.
  • Accuracy check — hold A on SET TARGET, lay it flat, press A, then stand it on a known angle and read to 0.1°. Printable wedges if you don't own an angle block.
  • Time on-angle on the session summary, and your last five sessions (hold B on the summary).
  • Fixes: session time survives sleep; a crash or reflash no longer brings back the previous knife as RESUME; Plus2 / S3 no longer treat the wake press as "off."

Want to measure the filter on your own hardware? The diagnostic builds are still available.
